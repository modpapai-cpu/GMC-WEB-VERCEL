PURCHASE & REFUND INFORMATION

REFUND & CANCELLATION

Because GMC products are digital, cancellation and refund requests are handled according to the status of the order and whether the digital product or credentials have already been delivered.

1. Before Successful Payment

If payment has not been successfully completed, no order delivery takes place. Customers may simply stop the payment process.

2. Payment Successful but Delivery Failed

If your payment was successfully captured but you did not receive the purchased digital product or credentials, contact GMC support with your order details. We will verify the payment and attempt to resolve the delivery issue or provide an appropriate remedy.

3. After Digital Delivery

Once a license key or account credentials have been successfully delivered, refunds may be restricted because the digital item can no longer be treated like an unused physical product. Any request will be reviewed based on the circumstances of the order.

4. Duplicate or Incorrect Charges

If you believe you were charged more than once for the same order, contact GMC with the relevant payment/order reference. Verified duplicate payments will be investigated and handled appropriately.

5. Cancellation

A purchase cannot always be cancelled after payment verification or credential reservation. If you need to cancel, contact support as soon as possible and before the digital item is delivered where possible.

6. How to Request Help

Send your order/payment reference, purchase email and a short description of the issue through the GMC contact page. Do not send your full card number, CVV, UPI PIN or other sensitive payment credentials.

7. Processing

Refunds, where approved, are normally returned through the payment method or process supported by the payment provider and may take additional processing time outside GMC control.

Important: These pages provide the website's current policies for GMC digital products. If you have an order-specific question, please contact GMC support before making assumptions about eligibility or delivery.
