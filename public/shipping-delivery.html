DIGITAL DELIVERY POLICY

SHIPPING & DELIVERY

GMC products are delivered electronically. No physical package is normally shipped for digital products purchased through this website.

1. Delivery Method

Purchased digital products are delivered electronically using the email address provided during checkout and/or the download link shown as part of the order flow.

2. Delivery Timing

After payment is successfully verified, GMC processes the order and sends the applicable digital delivery. Delivery is generally automated, but technical or payment-provider delays can occasionally occur.

3. What You May Receive

Depending on the selected plan, delivery may include a license key, username and password, a download link, order information, or a combination of these items.

4. Customer Responsibility

Please enter a valid email address and check your inbox, spam, promotions and other filtered folders after purchase. Keep delivered credentials private and secure.

5. Payment Pending

If a payment is pending or has not been successfully verified, the order may not be delivered yet. Do not make repeated payments unless the payment status confirms that the previous attempt failed.

6. Delivery Problems

If you have completed payment but have not received the delivery, contact GMC support with your order/payment reference and purchase email. We will verify the transaction and assist with delivery.

7. Physical Shipping

GMC does not normally ship physical goods under the digital product offerings covered by this policy. Therefore, shipping charges and physical courier tracking generally do not apply.

Important: These pages provide the website's current policies for GMC digital products. If you have an order-specific question, please contact GMC support before making assumptions about eligibility or delivery.
