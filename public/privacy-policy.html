PRIVACY POLICY

This Privacy Policy explains how GMC may collect, use, store and protect information when you use our website, purchase a digital product, or contact our support team.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide during checkout or support requests, such as your name, email address, contact number, order details and payment-related reference information. Payment credentials such as card, UPI or banking details are processed by our payment provider and are not intentionally stored by GMC.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to process orders, deliver purchased digital products, send transaction or delivery emails, provide customer support, prevent abuse or fraudulent activity, maintain service records, and improve the website.

3. Digital Product Credentials

Where a purchase includes a license key or username and password, those credentials are provided to the customer for the purchased service. Customers should keep them confidential and should not publish or share them publicly.

4. Payments & Third Parties

Payments may be processed through Razorpay and transactional emails may be delivered through third-party email infrastructure. Those providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse or disclosure. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain order and support information for as long as reasonably necessary for order fulfillment, customer support, accounting, security, dispute resolution and legal or regulatory requirements.

7. Cookies & Similar Technologies

The website may use essential browser storage, cookies or similar technologies to maintain sessions, remember preferences, operate checkout and improve functionality.

8. Your Choices

You may contact GMC to request clarification about information associated with your order or to raise a privacy concern. Some information may need to be retained where required for legitimate business, security or legal purposes.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this policy when our services, technology or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page.

Important: These pages provide the website's current policies for GMC digital products. If you have an order-specific question, please contact GMC support before making assumptions about eligibility or delivery.
